About Small Steps

Small Steps was created for people who want to improve without feeling overwhelmed. Instead of trying to change everything at once, the site encourages one repeatable habit per day.

The 100-day format gives visitors a clear finish line. A person can read, walk, stretch, clean, write, save money, or practice another small habit and record a simple reflection afterward.

How to Use This Site

  1. Choose one habit from the Habits page.
  2. Complete the habit today.
  3. Write and Save a short reflection on the Home page.
  4. Review your progress on the Progress page.

Why Small Habits Help

Small habits are useful because they lower the pressure to be perfect. A habit like reading for 10 minutes, walking around the block, or writing one paragraph in your journal is easier to repeat than a large goal with far too many steps.

When the action is simple, place your focus on consistency. Over time, those repeated choices can support confidence, discipline, and personal growth.

I remember starting 100 days ago. At first, it wasn't easy, but I pushed forward with small simple actions. I remember when I hit my first milestone at 20 days, I wanted to give up, and I almost did. But I kept going, even when I didn't feel like it, I pushed forward. It wasn't what I was doing that mattered, but the consistency of the action. The more consistent I was, the more exciting it became. Now, 100 days later, I'm not sure I recognize the person I was 100 days ago. Start small, do your best. If you fall behind, start again. Right now, you have the power to change the entire direction of your life.
